## Initial and final AI judgments

Both initial rating sets filled every item but kept completion/resolution flags false until comparison. That withheld finalization does not mean the item-level judgments were missing. The separate final copy has both flags true after every disagreement was explicitly classified and both AI contexts agreed. Semantic uncertainty still counts as failure.

The final changes relative to evaluator A were:

1. O004 nonlifting-today proposition: uncertain → omitted, because today is explicitly absent.
2. O009 suspension support: uncertain → absent/unglossed; the actual right-hand suspension remains unexplained.
3. O011 solution support: inaccurate → absent/unglossed; the colored-solution term remains unexplained while dissolved wording occupies the wrong participant role.
4. O013 pivot support: absent/unglossed → uncertain, recognizing a partial but nondistinguishing generic support description.
5. O014 gear support: supported → uncertain, because toothed-wheel structure and incoming drive do not express outgoing motion transfer.

The other 165 categorical decisions agreed initially. All six unsupported claims agreed substantively; evidence-range/rationale wording differed. Final source-aware judgments do not overwrite the output-only answers. Their later source comparison is a separately labeled analysis.
